Right. The hero comes at dawn, as always, sword first, questions later. I have kept this throne warm for 400 years, and the cushions reek of smoke. The magic kettle leaks since the war. The crown itches. It always itches. My ravens bring the same news: the west farms paid the grain tithe, the river witches want a bridge, my knee predicts rain. The prophecy says I fall at the seventh bell, so I wind the clock back each night. Listen, if you must slay me, do it before lunch. After lunch I nap, and the kingdom, frankly, runs itself.
